The tube groupoid

Braids on a tube: a front bed of strands (red) and a back bed (blue), joined by two seams. An object is a pair (f, b) — how many strands each bed holds. The word below the start object is read top-to-bottom: each generator is one vertical unit of braid.

Generators (positive form; - is the inverse)

  • f0+ f1+ … — crossing of adjacent front strands. Front strands are numbered F₁ … F_f from page-left to page-right; the index is 0-based, so fi+ crosses Fi+1 and Fi+2 (f0+ crosses the two leftmost).
  • b0+ b1+ … — crossing of adjacent back strands. Back strands are numbered B₁ … B_b from page-right to page-left, so b0+ crosses the two rightmost.
  • L+ — the leftmost front strand rides up around the left seam and appends at the left end of the back bed.
  • R+ — the rightmost back strand rides around the right seam and appends at the right end of the front bed.
  • Sign rule: positive crossings are left-over-right from every viewpoint (both from inside the annulus and from outside). Bare L, R mean L+, R+.

Relations

Juxtaposition means “do the left one first”. Index bounds are implied (a relation is imposed wherever both sides parse).

  • fi f(i+1) fi = f(i+1) fi f(i+1) — braid; same for b
  • fi fj = fj fi, |i−j| ≥ 2 — distant crossings; same for b
  • fi bj = bj fi — the beds commute
  • f(i+1) L+ = L+ fi,   bj L+ = L+ bj — L bookkeeping
  • fi R+ = R+ fi,   b(j+1) R+ = R+ bj — R bookkeeping
  • L+ R+ = R+ L+ — conveyor
  • f0 L+ L+ = L+ L+ b⟨b⟩ — left seam slide; ⟨b⟩ = back count at the start
  • b0 R+ R+ = R+ R+ f⟨f⟩ — right seam slide; ⟨f⟩ = front count at the start

Controls

  • Left-click drag — pan the view (look around).
  • W / ↑ — fly up; S / ↓ — fly down (capped by the word’s length).
  • A / ← and D / → — move left / right along x, relative to where you are looking (capped by the braid’s width). Movement accelerates the longer you hold a key.
  • V or the view button — teleport between the annulus view (camera inside the tube) and the front view (camera outside, on the front side — classic braid-diagram reading; the outline gaps show which strand crosses over). Only the camera’s z changes: your position and look direction are kept.
  • Word box — edits re-render live. Whitespace is ignored, generators are case-insensitive, # comments to the end of the line. The two number entries pick the starting object (f, b).
  • 🔗 Share — copies a link to the clipboard that reproduces the current word, starting object, view, camera position and look direction.

The preloaded demo word walks through every relation: each line multiplies a relation’s left-hand side by the inverse of its right-hand side, so the whole word is the identity — just written in a complicated way.
